## Source-derived NV012 requirements now represented

- Working proof of concept plus feasibility report; report alone is insufficient.
- End-to-end ingest → retrieve → grounded answer → source citation.
- Publicly releasable representative proxy corpus in Phase I; do not assume government-furnished data.
- SME-reviewed evaluation set and baseline against a general-purpose LLM.
- Metrics include unsupported-claim rate, citation accuracy, retrieval precision/recall, and expert-judged correctness.
- Retention and transfer are primary learning outcomes; faster proficiency is secondary.
- Schoolhouse is preferred early test case; fleet/on-the-job is longer-term.
- Instructor retains intervention, progression, and attrition decisions.
- Minimum provenance includes document, section, version, and retrieved passage.
- Detect superseded instructions and source conflicts.
- Plan for future controlled-data handling and markings; Phase I is unclassified.
- STTR research institution leads and conducts R&D.
- No single transition host is specified.
